Shardly/ engineering

How a document becomes searchable.

Shardly extracts text, commits it to disk, and builds an index. A search result points back to the document and the revision that produced it.

A source-backed engineering guide. Hover or focus a rail mark to preview a chapter.

Keep the text. Keep its identity.

The source file and its searchable document have different roles. A PDF provides extracted fields, not a complete reconstruction of its page layout.

A stable record key identifies the document during an update. Its source namespace tells the engine which import owns it.

Know what extraction preserves.

Office parsers keep useful structures such as headings, slides, and sheet cells. Optional OCR extracts text from page images. It does not return word coordinates or a complete visual layout.
